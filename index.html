Every UK-listed return of value, fetched from the filings.

ROVR reads the National Storage Mechanism each day and turns tender offer announcements into comparable precedents: size, percentage of shares sought, fixed or range pricing, premium to the undisturbed price, and the final take-up.

ROVR reads the National Storage Mechanism each day and turns B share scheme announcements into comparable precedents: size, amount per share, how the B shares are issued and redeemed, the options given to shareholders, and any share consolidation.

ROVR reads the National Storage Mechanism each day and turns reduction of capital announcements into comparable precedents: what was cancelled, whether cash was returned or distributable reserves created, the amount per share, and the court confirmation and effective dates.

ROVR reads the National Storage Mechanism each day and turns special dividend announcements into comparable precedents: amount per share and in total, whether it is a one-off return or part of a recurring policy, any share consolidation, and the ex, record and payment dates.

Premium is as stated by the issuer and the reference price varies (last close, VWAP or top of a price range); a negative figure is a discount, usual where a fund tenders at a discount to net asset value. Values are shown in the currency announced. Special dividend totals are as announced by the issuer; a starred date means the declaring announcement is not on the NSM and the record, payment or ex date is shown instead. Reduction of capital amounts are the share premium, capital or reserve cancelled as stated by the issuer; where cash was returned the amount returned is shown. B share scheme values are as announced and may cover only the capital element where an income alternative was offered. ROVR is a concept by M&A Monitor.
